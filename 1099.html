Quick notes

This is Copy B (for the recipient/cleaner). You need to give each cleaner their copy if you paid them $600+ during the year. Fill it out here, then hit Print to get a clean printout. The IRS copy (Copy A) must be filed electronically via IRS IRIS — do not mail a self-printed Copy A.
